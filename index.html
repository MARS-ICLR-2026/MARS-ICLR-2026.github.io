01 / THE IDEA

Task success is only
part of the story.

Completing a manipulation task can still disturb fragile objects, collide with obstacles, or cause an unsafe interaction. A language instruction alone does not reliably control the continuous actions of a robot.

MARS connects semantic risk understanding to action-space steering. A frozen vision-language model identifies scene risks. A frozen flow-matching policy then provides counterfactual velocity references, which MARS uses to construct a bounded correction to the original action chunk.

02 / THE METHOD

Two components.
One frozen policy.

Turn what the model sees into a correction
the robot can act on.

A
GRV

Grounded Risk Verbalization

A frozen Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct model reads agent and wrist views at 256 × 256 resolution. It filters non-target risks by grounding confidence and semantic relevance, ranks retained risks by severity, and expresses unwanted interactions as affirmative counterfactual commands. These commands supply references; they are never executed.

B
TAFR

Trajectory-Anchored Flow Repulsion

Compare nominal and counterfactual velocities at the same anchor state and flow time. Mask, clip, and severity-weight their residuals, then accumulate a separate correction without perturbing the anchor trajectory. Guidance varies across both the action horizon and the ODE integration steps.

Risk assessment on a schedule

GRV reuses risk records between calls, with a default interval of four replanning cycles. Potentially risky moving objects shorten the interval to one cycle. Assessment is pipelined with action execution; the number of retained risks is capped to keep the total guidance bounded.

WHERE STEERING IS HARDER

Moving obstacles remain challenging: for π0.5, MARS increases cumulative cost on DynamicObstacles at all three difficulty levels. Tight grasping tolerances can also limit the benefit of action corrections. Clipping bounds changes in normalized action coordinates; it does not certify collision-free motion. Missed hazards, stale risk records, and conflicting risk residuals remain limitations.
